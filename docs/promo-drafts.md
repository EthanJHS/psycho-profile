**제목**: 성격검사 하나 만들었는데 해보고 피드백 좀 줄 수 있어?

MBTI 말고 다른 걸 해보고 싶어서, 심리학 연구에서 쓰는 HEXACO 모델(6요인)로 성격검사를 직접 만들었어.

## English

### A. Reddit (r/personalitytests, r/SampleSize, r/SideProject — check each sub’s rules; some only allow self-promo on certain days)

**Title**: I built a free 48-question personality test based on HEXACO (22 archetypes) — would love honest feedback

I wanted something other than MBTI, so I built a test on the HEXACO model (six factors used in personality research) and mapped the results to 22 archetypes — The Sage, The Dreamer, The Cynic, The Playmaker, and so on.

- 48 questions, about 8 minutes
- No sign-up, no email, free
- You get your archetype, your six factor scores, and what sets you apart from others with the same archetype
- You can send a link to a friend and compare your factors side by side

It hasn’t been validated at scale yet, and I say so on the result page — the questions and archetypes are my own work built on the HEXACO model. I’m especially interested in whether the result feels accurate and whether any question reads oddly to a native speaker (it was first written in Korean).
